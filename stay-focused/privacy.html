Privacy Policy

Stay Focused · Effective date: November 7, 2026

Who we are

Stay Focused is published by Lucas Chiesa. Questions about this policy: lucaschiesa01@gmail.com.

Saved on your device

Your tasks, notes, focus history, streak and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, and we do not receive this content. It stays until you delete it or remove the app, and may be included in device backups managed by Apple or Google.

Ads

The free plan shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may process your IP address, device information, ad interactions and, if allowed, your advertising identifier. Your tasks and notes are never used for ads. On iOS the app asks for permission before tracking. Where the law requires it, the app asks for consent and offers ad privacy options in Settings. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps.

Purchases

Payments are handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your card details. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription, which processes an anonymous app ID, purchase history and basic device information. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Your choices and rights

You can delete blocks and notes in the app, or remove everything on your device by uninstalling it. Depending on where you live, including US states such as California, you may have rights to access, delete or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. We do not sell your tasks or notes. If personalized ads count as sharing under your local law, you can opt out using the options above. For any request, write to lucaschiesa01@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
